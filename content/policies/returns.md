# Product Return Policy

Customers may return unused Cerenity products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the purchase price, excluding shipping.

## Condition
Items must be unopened unless they are defective. Wearables (Pulse Band, Quiet Buds) that have been paired to an account cannot be restocked and are only eligible for replacement if they fail within 12 months.

## Supplements and beverages
Opened Calm Drops, Night Oil, Sleep Tea, Hydrate Mix, and Morning Blend cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Unopened bottles and tins are accepted.

## Process
Support Lead Chris Novak owns return tickets. Customers email support@cerenity.test with the SKU and order number. Refunds post in 5 business days after warehouse intake.

## Warranty
Aura Mat and Stretch Bands have a 24-month manufacturing warranty. Desk Light has a 12-month warranty. Hardware PM Sam Ortiz approves out-of-policy replacements.
