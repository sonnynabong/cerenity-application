# Remote Work Policy

Cerenity supports remote work for full-time employees whose roles do not require on-site hardware labs.

## Core hours
Teams overlap 10:00–15:00 in their local timezone for meetings. Async updates are the default outside that window.

## Office access
Manila, Singapore, Austin, and London offices remain hot-desks. Remote employees may book a desk up to two days per week without manager approval.

## Equipment
Cerenity ships a laptop and a Quiet Buds headset (SKU CER-111) to remote hires. Desk Light (SKU CER-108) is optional and must be requested through People Ops.

## Travel
Remote employees visit a hub office once per quarter. Travel is booked through the expense policy; flights over six hours require economy-plus, not business class.
