# Paid Time Off

Full-time Cerenity employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, plus local public holidays at their home location.

## Accrual
PTO is granted on January 1. New hires receive a pro-rated bank in their first year (full days only). Unused days roll over up to 5 days; anything above 5 is forfeited on March 31.

## Approval
Requests of 3 days or fewer can be approved by a manager in Slack. Longer leave needs 14 days of notice and a backup owner listed in the calendar event.

## Wellness days
Employees may take 2 unplanned wellness days per quarter without using PTO. These cannot be combined with a Friday-Monday weekend to create a four-day holiday without manager approval.

## People Ops
Alex Rivera in People Ops owns PTO balances. Do not ask Finance for remaining days.
