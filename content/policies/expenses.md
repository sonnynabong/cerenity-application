# Expense Policy

Cerenity reimburses reasonable business costs that have a receipt and a Cerenity purpose written on the claim.

## Limits
- Meals while traveling: 60 USD per person per meal, 120 USD for client dinners.
- Local transit and rideshare: no cap, but trips over 80 USD need a manager note.
- Software subscriptions: must be billed to a company card after Legal review. Dana Okonkwo signs vendor terms.

## Wellness products
Employees may expense one personal Cerenity product per year at 50% off list price. Pulse Band (CER-104) and Quiet Buds (CER-111) are excluded from the discount because of limited stock.

## Submission
File claims in the finance portal within 30 days. Riley Cho is the Finance Partner who rejects claims without receipts.

## Prohibited
Alcohol above one drink at a client meal, first-class flights, and gifts over 25 USD are not reimbursable.
